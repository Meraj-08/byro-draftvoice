# Evidence

What Rico and Fathin actually write on LinkedIn, labelled so DraftVoice can use it.

## How it was collected

- **Sources:** Rico's activity page (Comments) and a search of his posts; Fathin's activity page (Comments) and a search of his posts. All viewed 2 Oct 2026 in my normal browser.
- **Method:** I scrolled the page and copied its text by hand (select all, copy). No scraper, script, or extension. An AI assistant split the copy into comments; I checked the labels.
- **Dates:** LinkedIn shows relative times ("3h", "2d", "1w"). Dates below are counted back from 2 Oct 2026, so "1w" and "2w" are approximate (~).
- **Privacy:** Rico's comments are copied exactly. Other people's posts are summarised in my words, and their names are replaced with `[person]`.
- **Deduplication:** the page repeats the parent post above every reply, so each comment is counted once.
- **Coverage:** about 145 Rico comments from 7 Sep to 2 Oct 2026 (31 on other people's posts, about 114 replies on his own posts), and 30 of his posts from Dec 2025 to 2 Oct 2026. Fathin: 18 comments (Apr to Jul 2026; nothing newer was shown) and 18 posts (Dec 2025 to 1 Oct 2026). Dates without a year are 2026.
